About Wanderlust Travels

Exploring the world, one destination at a time since 2005.

Our Story

Founded in 2005 by seasoned traveler Elena Marquez, Wanderlust Travels was born from a passion for discovering hidden gems across the globe. What started as a small travel blog has grown into a full-service travel recommendation company trusted by over 500,000 travelers worldwide. Our team of 50+ travel experts has collectively visited over 120 countries, ensuring that every recommendation we make comes from firsthand experience. We believe travel isn't just about destinations — it's about the stories you bring home.

Our Top Travel Recommendations

🏖️ Bali, Indonesia

Best for: Culture & Relaxation

Explore ancient temples, lush rice terraces, and world-class beaches. Our experts recommend staying in Ubud for culture and Seminyak for nightlife.

From $1,200 / person

🏔️ Swiss Alps, Switzerland

Best for: Adventure & Scenery

Whether you ski in winter or hike in summer, the Swiss Alps offer breathtaking views year-round. Don't miss the Jungfrau region and Lake Lucerne.

From $2,500 / person

🏛️ Kyoto, Japan

Best for: History & Cuisine

Walk through thousands of vermillion torii gates at Fushimi Inari, visit serene Zen gardens, and savor authentic kaiseki dining.

From $1,800 / person

🌊 Santorini, Greece

Best for: Romance & Photography

Famous for its white-washed buildings and stunning sunsets over the caldera. Perfect for honeymoons and couples looking for a magical getaway.

From $1,600 / person

🦁 Serengeti, Tanzania

Best for: Wildlife & Safari

Witness the Great Migration, spot the Big Five, and sleep under the African stars in luxury tented camps. A once-in-a-lifetime experience.

From $3,200 / person

🌮 Mexico City, Mexico

Best for: Food & Art

From the murals of Diego Rivera to the street tacos of Coyoacán, Mexico City is a vibrant blend of ancient history and modern creativity.

From $900 / person
